Questions

Is the Editor really free?

Yes, always. The Kerf Editor is free and open source (AGPL-3.0). Anyone can use it, without signing in. A school account adds saved files, groups and Pupil Cards: that part is a free trial until September 2027.

Which lasers can we use?

Any laser whose own software opens DXF or SVG files. Export your design from the Kerf Editor and open it in the software you already use (LightBurn, LaserGRBL and others).

Do pupils need email addresses?

No. Pupils can sign in with group codes: a school code, a group code, their pupil ID and a two-word password. These are printed on the Pupil Card from their teacher.

How is pupils' data handled?

Kerf's logs carry pupil IDs, never names. Your school controls its own accounts. The Kerf team can see your school's data only with your school's permission. Kerf is hosted by mr-salih.org.

Can we use our Google or Microsoft accounts?

Soon. Each school switches on the sign-in methods it wants. Today that is DINO, email and password for staff, and Pupil Cards for pupils; Google and Microsoft are coming.

What happens after September 2027?

The Editor stays free. For the classroom features, we will tell every school in good time, well before September 2027. Nothing is deleted.
