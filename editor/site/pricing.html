Questions

Is the Editor really free?

Yes. The Kerf Editor is free and open source (AGPL-3.0). Anyone can use it, even without signing in. A free school account adds saved files, groups and Pupil Cards.

What lasers work?

Kerf Cutter currently supports the Creality Falcon. Kerf Cutter is a small program that runs on the PC next to each laser and connects it to Kerf.

Do pupils need email addresses?

No. Pupils can sign in with group codes: a school code, a group code, their pupil ID and a two-word password. These are printed on the Pupil Card from their teacher.

How is pupils' data handled?

Kerf's logs carry pupil IDs, never names. Your school controls its own accounts. The Kerf team can see your school's data only with your school's permission. Kerf is hosted by mr-salih.org.

Can we use our Google or Microsoft accounts?

Yes. Each school switches on the sign-in methods it wants: DINO, Google, Microsoft, email links for staff, and group codes for pupils.

What happens when an offer ends?

Nothing is deleted. The extras just stop.
