How it works

From a pupil's idea to a file ready to cut, in three steps.

  1. 1

    Pupils design

    Pupils draw their design in the Kerf Editor. It runs in the browser on school computers, so there is nothing to install.

  2. 2

    Work is saved in Kerf

    With a school account, pupils' files are saved as they draw and follow them to any school computer when they sign in.

  3. 3

    Export and cut

    Export the design as a DXF or SVG file and cut it with your school's own laser software.

What is Kerf

Two parts: the Editor, and Kerf for classrooms.

The Kerf Editor Free, always

Laser design software that runs in the browser. Anyone can use it, with or without an account.

  • The Editor tools

    Shapes, text, booleans, offsets, finger-joint boxes, box nets, image tracing, DXF import, and DXF and SVG export.

  • No account needed

    Open the Editor in the browser and start. Your work stays in this browser, and you can export it for any laser.

  • Free and open source

    The Editor is open source (AGPL-3.0), so it stays free for every pupil and every school.

Kerf for classrooms Free trial until September 2027

Your school's own Kerf: staff and pupil accounts, groups, Pupil Cards and saved files. Register your school to try it.

  • Groups and Pupil Cards

    Pupils join groups with a code, and a pupil can be in several groups. Print a card for each pupil with their sign-in details.

  • Files saved in Kerf

    Pupils' work is saved as they draw, and follows them to any school computer when they sign in.

  • Works with your school's sign-in

    Sign in with DINO (Google and Microsoft are coming). Staff use their school email and a password, and pupils use their Pupil Cards.

Safety and privacy

Safe for children. Your school stays in control.

Kerf is hosted by mr-salih.org.

  • Pupil IDs, not names

    Kerf's logs carry pupil IDs. They never carry pupils' names.

  • School-controlled accounts

    Your school makes, changes and removes its own pupil and staff accounts.

  • Support only with your permission

    The Kerf team can see your school's data only when your school gives permission.
